Dear reader,

You wrote to say something I have heard, in some form, from almost every person who has ever stayed after one of these talks. *I do not know when I stopped being present. I only know that I have.*

I want to write back honestly.

> The opposite of disconnection is not engagement. It is noticing.

The work, when you decide to do it, is not loud. It is closer to remembering than to building.

Yours,
Tristian
