There is a ritual I do in the empty room before the audience walks in. It is not a warm-up. It is closer to a question I ask myself, three times, in a specific order.

## 1. Who is in the front row?

Not their job title. Their *Tuesday*. The keynote that lands is the one written for the person who almost did not come.

## 2. What did they pay to feel?

Not what they paid to hear. Not what they paid to learn. What they paid to *feel*, by minute forty.

## 3. What part of this talk would I cut if my mother were in the room?

> The most important question I ask before a keynote has nothing to do with the talk.

Whatever survives the third question is the talk.
